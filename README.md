A single 1024×1024 broadsheet image summing up the world news of Sunday
22 November 2026: a map-led lead story (Iran widens the Gulf war — two
supertankers mined off Hormuz, Bahrain plant hit, US blockade at day
130) with 13 numbered markers tied to the story cards, twelve
illustrated story cards with numbered badges, a twelve-item briefs
panel and a six-item news ticker — 31 stories across 7 regions.

Headlines drawn from live wire reporting on 22 Nov 2026 (Reuters, AP,
CNN, Formula1.com, UKMTO and others): the COP31 climate summit ending
in collapse with no deal after 50 hours of overtime, Verstappen's Las
Vegas GP win (+0.639s over Norris, 35 lead changes), the Kryvyi Rih
triple-tap strike, a drone collapsing a Kyiv-region apartment block,
the Sparta II fire in the Gulf of Finland, the AI market rout, the
capture of "El Chapo Isidro", Peru's M6.7 earthquake, the Cikande
candy-factory fire in Indonesia, the US losing measles elimination
status, Manipur's toxic liquor deaths and the Air Niugini emergency
landing in Davao.

## Build

- `generate.py` renders `entry.png` from `stories.json` with Pillow.
- `main.py` is the make.fun entrypoint; output goes to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set, otherwise beside the script.
